AI Agents That Work for Your Business

Imagine having a digital team that can handle repetitive tasks, respond to customers, organize information, and support your business around the clock. Our AI agents are designed to integrate into your business workflows and assist your team with everyday operations.

1. AI Customer Support Agent

Deliver faster customer support without increasing your workload.

  • Answer frequently asked customer questions.
  • Provide instant responses to website enquiries.
  • Handle common customer service requests.
  • Collect customer information and enquiries.
  • Escalate complex requests to your team.
  • Provide 24/7 automated assistance.

Ideal for: Retail stores, service businesses, restaurants, e-commerce, and professional services.

2. AI Sales & Lead Generation Agent

Never miss an opportunity to connect with a potential customer.

  • Capture leads from your website.
  • Engage visitors through intelligent conversations.
  • Qualify potential customers.
  • Collect contact information.
  • Send automated follow-up messages.
  • Organize and forward qualified leads to your sales team.

Ideal for: Real estate agencies, consultants, agencies, contractors, and B2B businesses.

3. AI Business Assistant

Your intelligent digital assistant for everyday business operations.

  • Organize business information.
  • Draft emails and customer responses.
  • Summarize documents and reports.
  • Assist with scheduling and administrative tasks.
  • Retrieve information from approved business documents.
  • Support internal team workflows.

Ideal for: Small businesses, entrepreneurs, managers, and administrative teams.

4. AI Appointment & Booking Agent

Make appointment management easier for your business and customers.

  • Respond to booking enquiries.
  • Check available appointment slots through connected systems.
  • Schedule and manage appointments.
  • Send confirmations and reminders.
  • Handle rescheduling and cancellation requests.
  • Reduce manual booking administration.

Ideal for: Clinics, salons, consultants, automotive services, and local businesses.

5. AI Marketing & Social Media Agent

Make your marketing workflow faster and more consistent.

  • Generate social media content ideas.
  • Draft Facebook and Instagram posts.
  • Create marketing campaign concepts.
  • Generate promotional content.
  • Assist with customer engagement.
  • Prepare content calendars and campaign reports.

Ideal for: Small businesses, marketing agencies, e-commerce, and local brands.

6. Custom AI Agents

Have a unique business process that needs automation? We design custom AI agents around your specific requirements.

  • AI document processing agents.
  • Invoice and receipt processing agents.
  • Internal knowledge-base assistants.
  • Customer enquiry management agents.
  • Email management and response agents.
  • Inventory and order assistance.
  • Multi-agent business automation systems.
